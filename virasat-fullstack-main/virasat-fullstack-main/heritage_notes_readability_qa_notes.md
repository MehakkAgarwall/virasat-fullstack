# Heritage Notes readability repair QA

The reported issue affected the three ivory editorial cards in Heritage Notes. Their body copy was inheriting a low-contrast presentation, and the card headings/actions were also being overridden by a late light-text treatment.

The cards now use a warm ivory surface with deep forest title text, dark green body copy, antique-gold eyebrow labels, and clearly underlined antique-gold actions. Browser verification confirms all three note titles, paragraphs, and links are visually legible while the route, material, and maker imagery stays unchanged.

Full quality gates passed: **42 tests across 21 files**, TypeScript validation, and production build. Managed-storage resolution notices and the JavaScript chunk-size advisory remain non-blocking build output only.

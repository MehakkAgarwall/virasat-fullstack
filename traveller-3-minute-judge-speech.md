# Virāsat — Traveller Role Demo Speech

> **Approximate duration:** Three minutes at a natural presentation pace.

Good morning, respected judges. I am presenting **Virāsat** from the perspective of a Traveller.

When we travel across India, we usually see monuments, hotels, and famous destinations. But we often miss the living heritage that exists just a few kilometres away: the weaver at a loom, the maker of a traditional toy, the story behind a textile, or a workshop that keeps a community’s craft alive. Virāsat makes these cultural experiences visible, credible, and easier to include in a real journey.

Let me begin as a Traveller. On the home screen, Virāsat invites me to trace a cultural trail. Instead of planning only from one city to another, I can enter my origin and destination and discover craft traditions that lie along or close to my route.

For example, when I plan a journey through Karnataka, Virāsat can surface the Mysuru Silk tradition. The Traveller experience does not simply show a generic tourism card. It connects the route to a craft, the craft to its cultural story, and where verified information exists, to the maker and a managed cultural experience.

Here, I can open the Mysore Silk Weaving Experience. I can understand the location, the duration, available time options, the Artisan identity, and the cultural context before deciding to visit. The platform is designed to be source-honest: it does not pretend that every craft has a workshop, price, or booking slot. If information is not verified, Virāsat shows cultural context without falsely calling it bookable.

When an experience is available, I select a date and time and click **Book this experience**. This is not just a visual button. The booking is saved in the application database and linked to both sides of the journey. I can open **My Bookings** and see my request and its current status.

Now the important part is the two-way connection. When I switch to the Artisan workspace, the same booking appears in the Artisan’s Booking Inbox. The Artisan can accept or decline the request. Once accepted, the Traveller ledger automatically reflects the updated status. This makes the journey reliable for both the Traveller and the maker.

Virāsat also helps me explore beyond a single booking. The **Craft Atlas** maps live craft records across India, while **My Journey** gives me a personal space for my trails, notes, reflections, and shared heritage stories. If I choose to share, I can contribute to a wider digital board of Traveller experiences while keeping private entries private by default.

In short, Virāsat transforms travel from simply reaching a destination into meeting the living culture along the way. For the Traveller, it offers discovery, trust, and a meaningful connection. For artisans, it creates visibility and real engagement. And for tourism authorities, it provides a clearer view of cultural routes and traveller interest.

Thank you.
